How You Can Contribute

The Satya Yuga Bhumi Trust is a collective effort. We welcome individuals from all walks of life to contribute their unique skills and resources to build a new paradigm for humanity. Here are the primary ways you can participate:

1. Land Donors

Secure your land's legacy by placing it into the Bhumi Trust.

As a Land Donor, you take the profound step of removing your land from the speculative market, preserving it for eternity under the stewardship of the community.

Begin the Process

2. Sevaks (Physical Stewards)

Join a physical community and contribute your service (Seva).

Sevaks are the hands-on guardians of the land. By joining one of our eco-villages, you contribute your skills in organic farming, natural building, and community well-being.

Find a Community (Coming Soon)

3. Digital Guardians

Manage the DAO remotely and shape its future.

Digital Guardians are the backbone of our decentralized governance. Contribute your expertise in software development, governance, legal frameworks, and more.
