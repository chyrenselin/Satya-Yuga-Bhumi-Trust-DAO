# Satya Yuga Bhumi Trust (DAO)

This repository contains the blueprint for the Satya Yuga Bhumi Trust, a Decentralized Autonomous Organization (DAO) focused on removing land from government and market ownership and placing it permanently under a community Bhumi Trust.

## Vision

To co-create a world where our collective existence serves the highest good of Earth and all its inhabitants, guided by universal principles of truth and dharma.

## Structure

The website is structured into several key sections:

- **Home:** Explains the core vision and purpose of Satyug.
- **How It Works:** Provides a plain-language explanation of the process from land to community stewardship.
- **DAO:** Details the non-monetary DAO governance model, token purpose, and lessons from historical failures.
- **Bhumi Trust:** Outlines the legal and philosophical framework for land stewardship.
- **Participate:** Explains how individuals can contribute through time, skills, and service, rather than money.
- **FAQ:** Addresses common questions about the project.
- **Vishwadharma:** Presents a universal dharma transcending traditional divisions.
- **Vishwasamvidhan:** Describes a universal constitution for all living and non-living beings.
- **Contracts:** Future voting system (undeveloped)
